HiMu: Hierarchical Multimodal Frame Selection for Long Video Question Answering ↗

Dan Ben-Ami, Gabriele Serussi, Kobi Cohen, Chaim Baskin

A training-free hierarchical multimodal frame selection framework for long-video QA that decomposes queries into logic trees with specialized experts, advancing the efficiency–accuracy Pareto front while requiring ~10× fewer FLOPs than agentic systems.

CVPR · 2026

HERBench: A Benchmark for Multi-Evidence Integration in Video Question Answering ↗

Dan Ben-Ami, Gabriele Serussi, Kobi Cohen, Chaim Baskin

A VideoQA benchmark for multi-evidence integration across time, requiring aggregating multiple non-overlapping evidential cues. Evaluating 13 SOTA Video-LLMs reveals pervasive failures (31–42% accuracy vs. 20% random baseline), disentangled into retrieval and fusion deficits.
